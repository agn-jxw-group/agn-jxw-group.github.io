bio: Zhen-Yi received his B.S. in Physics from Xiamen University in 2007, and his Ph.D. degrees in Astrophysics from the International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA), Italy, and in Theoretical Physics from Xiamen University in 2013 and 2014, respectively. He subsequently joined the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) as a postdoctoral fellow in April 2014, became a Specially Appointed Associate Researcher in November 2016, was appointed Associate Professor in April 2020, and was appointed Specially Appointed Professor in December 2025. He teaches courses including "Electromagnetism" for undergraduates and "Radiative Processes in Astrophysics" for graduate students. His research focuses on theoretical and observational studies of active galactic nucleus (AGN) radiation, black hole mass growth, and galaxy evolution.
