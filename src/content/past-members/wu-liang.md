Liang's research investigates the central engine, circumnuclear environment, and host co-evolution of active galactic nuclei. His work with the group used multi-wavelength observations to map the three-dimensional geometry of the dusty torus and ionization cones (Wu et al. 2023), revealed the connection between accretion variability and equatorial obscuration (Wu et al. 2024), and developed kinematically consistent line decomposition to trace quasar host star formation (Wu et al. 2026). His ongoing research continues to explore the observational connections between circumnuclear dust, ionized outflows, and galaxy-scale feedback. Liang is now a lecturer at [Chizhou University](https://www.czu.edu.cn) ([Personal Website](https://lwoo1999.github.io)).
