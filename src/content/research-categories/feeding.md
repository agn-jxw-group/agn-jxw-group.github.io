shortHeading: Feeding the giant
question: What happens to matter on its final journey into a supermassive black hole?
order: 1
introduction: >-
  An active galactic nucleus is powered by a supermassive black hole feeding on surrounding gas. The gas does not fall straight in: it settles into a luminous accretion disk, where the release of gravitational energy produces intense optical and ultraviolet light. Compact regions of energetic particles—the warm and hot coronae—transform part of that energy into X-rays, while some systems also launch powerful jets. By combining observations across the electromagnetic spectrum, we investigate how matter moves inward and how the released energy emerges as radiation and outflows.
