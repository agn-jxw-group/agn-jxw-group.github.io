shortHeading: Black holes and galaxies
question: How does a tiny central engine connect to an entire galaxy?
order: 3
introduction: >-
  Although tiny compared with a galaxy, an accreting supermassive black hole can release enough radiation, winds, and jets to influence gas across its host and beyond. These outflows may heat, compress, or expel gas and thereby alter future star formation, but demonstrating that causal connection observationally is difficult. We map dusty structures and ionization cones around active nuclei, relate their geometry to evolutionary stage, and build the evidence needed to determine when nuclear activity becomes genuine galaxy-scale feedback.
