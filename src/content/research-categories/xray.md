introduction: >-
  X-rays reveal matter heated to millions of degrees, particles accelerated by magnetic fields, and gas moving through environments ruled by extreme gravity. Beyond active galactic nuclei, we use surveys and pointed observations to study X-ray binaries, galaxy clusters, supernova remnants, the hot structure of the Milky Way, and rapidly changing transients. By turning detected photons into images, spectra, light curves, and source catalogues, we trace the energetic processes that shape the Universe.
